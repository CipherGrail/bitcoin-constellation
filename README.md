# Bitcoin Constellation

### Making the invisible universe of Bitcoin visible

*An open invitation to explore Bitcoin's monetary history as a spatial universe.*

**What if we could step inside Bitcoin?**

Since January 2009, Bitcoin has been recording a public history of monetary issuance and transfers between transaction outputs. We can inspect blocks, trace transactions and plot charts. But what would that history look like if we could inhabit it?

We are building **Bitcoin Constellation**: a reproducible spatial interpretation of Bitcoin's monetary history, designed for exploration on screens, in immersive environments and, eventually, under a planetarium dome.

## A universe born from the blockchain

Imagine a hemisphere.

Each new Bitcoin block appears at its zenith. As new blocks arrive, older blocks move down the hemisphere's surface. Their successive positions form **one continuous, invisible spiral**: one strand, not a galaxy of arbitrary branches.

Bitcoin addresses are represented as stars. Their monetary balances determine their physical volumes and influence their light. Their colors express the age of the satoshis they contain. Their spatial locations emerge from weighted relationships to the blocks in which those satoshis were originally issued.

As bitcoins move and mingle, some stars remain near the hemisphere's historical envelope while others occupy its interior. The result is not an illustration drawn to look like a galaxy. It is a geography derived from explicit rules applied to Bitcoin data.

## Travel through history. Observe the present.

We want to watch the first stars appear, travel through successive halvings, and see the constellation change as bitcoins circulate. In historical playback, time can be accelerated to reveal the evolution of the whole universe. At the equilibrium following any completed block, the scene can be frozen and explored.

In live mode, new blocks will update that universe. Luminous impulses will evoke the transactions recorded in each block: sending stars grow dimmer when their balances fall; receiving stars brighten as theirs rise.

The experience should remain meaningful to someone encountering Bitcoin for the first time, while allowing specialists to investigate the underlying data.

## A work of art, and an instrument of inquiry

Bitcoin Constellation sits at the intersection of computation, mathematics, data visualization, astronomy, digital art and education.

Its ambition is not simply to make Bitcoin look spectacular. The visual rules must be documented, reproducible and distinguishable from the on-chain facts they interpret. In particular, satoshi-level attribution requires a transparent convention: the Ordinals FIFO model is an indexing convention, not an identity recorded directly by Bitcoin consensus.

The beauty, if we succeed, should emerge from the rigor.

## A spatial layer zero

Beyond the immersive experience, we see Bitcoin Constellation as **a foundational spatial layer for exploring Bitcoin**: a common representation on which further analytical tools and interpretations may be built. It does not alter Bitcoin's protocol.

Clusters and apparent galaxies may reveal concentrations and relationships. Additional, separately documented analytical layers could help examine mining activity, exchange-related flows, large holders, or publicly attributed institutions and state entities. Attribution would require external evidence, and would never be inferred from proximity alone. Bitcoin nodes, which are network infrastructure rather than on-chain addresses, would likewise require their own separately sourced layer.

**Patterns matter. So do exceptions.**

Recurring behavior may yield recognizable spatial configurations. Singular historical activity may leave other, unusual signatures. The early mining period, including patterns sometimes associated with Satoshi Nakamoto, is one possible subject for investigation—not a claim that such an identity can be established from a constellation.

We may find regularities that support existing explanations. We may discover anomalies that challenge them. We may also learn which apparent structures are artifacts of our own visualization model. Each is a reason to keep asking questions.

**The aim is not only to see Bitcoin, but to create a place where we might discover what we did not yet know to look for.**

## An invitation

Early prototypes are under development. They are experimental: synthetic demonstrations are not yet proof of an exhaustive, independently validated historical reconstruction.

We welcome criticism, research questions, reproducibility challenges, ideas for analytical layers, suggestions for immersive installations, and contributions to the work ahead.

This is **not a new cryptocurrency, not a token offering, and not a proposal to change Bitcoin's consensus rules**.

We intend to publish progress and limitations openly, step by step.

*Bitcoin Constellation — A universe written by the blockchain.*
